LUU PHAN HOI THAM DU VAO GOOGLE SHEETS

1. Tao Google Sheet moi. Mo Extensions > Apps Script.
2. Xoa noi dung mau trong Apps Script va dan toan bo noi dung tep google-sheets/Code.gs vao tep Code.gs, sau do bam Save.
3. Chon Deploy > New deployment. Chon loai Web app:
   - Execute as: Me.
   - Who has access: Anyone.
   Bam Deploy, cap quyen theo yeu cau cua Google va sao chep URL ket thuc bang /exec.
4. Mo file ThiepCuoi/wwwroot/js/rsvp-config.js va gan URL vua sao chep vao googleSheetsUrl, vi du:
   googleSheetsUrl: "https://script.google.com/macros/s/ID_CUA_BAN/exec"
5. Chay lai .\publish-github-pages.ps1 de tao lai docs, sau do sao chep noi dung ben trong docs vao repository GitHub Pages va push len GitHub.
6. Gui mot phan hoi thu va kiem tra sheet "Phan hoi". URL Web app la cong khai; khong dat thong tin bi mat trong URL hoac ma trang web.

Ma Apps Script hoan chinh nam trong tep google-sheets/Code.gs.
