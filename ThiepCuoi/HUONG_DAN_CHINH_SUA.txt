Tat ca thong tin chinh cua thiep nam trong file:
appsettings.json

1. Doi ten co dau, chu re
- Wedding:BrideName
- Wedding:GroomName

2. Doi nha trai, nha gai
- Wedding:GroomFamily
- Wedding:BrideFamily
- Wedding:GroomParents
- Wedding:BrideParents

3. Doi ngay gio
- Wedding:WeddingDate
- Wedding:DateLabel

4. Doi dia diem va ban do
- Wedding:Venue
- Wedding:Address

5. Doi hinh anh
- Wedding:HeroImage
- Wedding:PortraitImage
- Wedding:Gallery

Co the dung link anh online hoac duong dan anh trong wwwroot.
Vi du: /images/anh-cuoi-1.jpg

6. Doi tai khoan va ma QR mung cuoi
Sua trong Wedding:BankAccounts.

Moi tai khoan gom:
- Role: Co dau hoac Chu re
- BankCode: ma ngan hang VietQR, vi du MB, VCB, BIDV, TCB, ACB
- BankName: ten hien thi
- AccountNumber: so tai khoan
- AccountHolder: ten chu tai khoan
- TransferNote: noi dung chuyen khoan

Website tu tao anh QR bang VietQR theo BankCode va AccountNumber.
