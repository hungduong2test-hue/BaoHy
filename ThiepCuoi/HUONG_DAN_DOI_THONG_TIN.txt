CAC FILE DOI THONG TIN CHINH
================================

Tat ca nam trong thu muc:
Config

1. 00-loai-thiep.json
- Doi loai thiep: Thanh Hon, Vu Quy, Bao Hi...
- InvitationType: dong loai thiep hien trong THONG TIN LE CUOI.
- CeremonyAnnouncement: dong bao tin, vi du "LE THANH HON CUA CON CHUNG TOI".
- CeremonyLocationText: dong noi le duoc cu hanh tai dau.

2. 01-thong-tin-trang-dau.json
- Doi ten co dau, chu re, ngay hien tren man dau.
- Doi anh dau tien qua HeroProfiles.
- Trong HeroProfiles: ai ghi tren se hien truoc o trang dau.
- Doi album trong muc Gallery. Muon 10-30 anh thi them nhieu dong:
  {
    "Url": "/images/album/ten-anh.jpg",
    "Caption": "Chu thich anh"
  }

3. 02-thong-tin-tiec-cuoi.json
- Doi gio/ngay/thang/nam trong THONG TIN TIEC CUOI.
- Doi lich don khach, khai tiec trong Timeline.

4. 03-dia-diem-tiec-cuoi.json
- Doi Venue va Address cho muc "Tiec cuoi se to chuc tai".
- Google map lay dia chi theo Address.
- MapImage la anh ban do tinh dang hien thi. Neu doi dia chi, nen thay MapImage bang anh ban do moi hoac de rong de dung map Google theo Address.

5. 04-thong-tin-le-cuoi.json
- Doi thong tin ong ba, dia chi hai ben trong THONG TIN LE CUOI.
- Trong CeremonyFamilies: muc nao ghi tren se hien ben trai.
- Doi thong tin co dau/chu re trong CoupleProfiles.

6. 05-hop-mung-cuoi.json
- Doi tai khoan ngan hang trong Hop mung cuoi.
- AccountHolder nen trung ten co dau/chu re trong file 04-thong-tin-le-cuoi.json.
- BankCode dung ma ngan hang VietQR, vi du: MB, BIDV, VCB, VPB, TCB.

7. 06-cau-hinh-hieu-ung.json
- AutoScrollSpeed: toc do tu cuon trang, don vi pixel/giay.
  So cang lon thi cuon cang nhanh. Goi y: 120 cham vua, 145 mac dinh, 180 nhanh.
- AutoScrollDelay: thoi gian cho truoc khi bat dau tu cuon, don vi mili-giay.
  So cang nho thi bat dau cuon cang som. Goi y: 300 den 800.

LUU Y
================================
- Luu file JSON xong refresh lai web. Neu dang chay dotnet run, app se tu doc lai cau hinh.
- Anh nen dat trong: wwwroot/images/trang-dau hoac wwwroot/images/album
- Duong dan anh viet dang: /images/album/ten-anh.jpg
